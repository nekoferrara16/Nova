A rhythm that works for you

01 · Start with your accounts

Set an opening balance at the start of your chosen date. Import only transactions on or after that date. For credit cards and loans, enter the positive amount you owe.

02 · Make a weekly check-in

Download a CSV from your bank, choose its columns and date format, and review the preview. Positive amounts add money; negative amounts subtract it. Mark card payments and movements between your accounts as transfers. Use expense kind for refunds to offset spending.

03 · Give your money a plan

Set monthly category budgets and check your cash flow. Balances are calculated from opening balances and recorded transactions; they are not a live bank feed. Investment accounts track entered cash values, not live market prices.

Reports use USD and include transactions through today or the selected month's end, whichever comes first. An incomplete statement means an incomplete picture. Repeated imports use transaction IDs when mapped, otherwise date, description, amount, and occurrence; review overlapping statements carefully.
